Song Uploader

Song Uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner, the creator behind the YouTube channel @Samuraikid11 and the TikTok account @samurai_kid11.

It uploads the owner's own original music videos, with their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.
